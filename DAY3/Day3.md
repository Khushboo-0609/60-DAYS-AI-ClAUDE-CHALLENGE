# Day 3 – Role-Based Prompting

## Challenge
**ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge – Day 3**

## Objective

Learn how assigning different roles (personas) changes the quality, tone, and relevance of AI-generated responses.

---

## What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting is a technique where you assign a specific role to AI before asking a question. This helps the AI answer from the perspective of that role, producing more relevant and specialized responses.

---

## Prompt Used

You are an AI educator teaching complete beginners.

Explain Role-Based Prompting in simple language.

Include:
- What Role-Based Prompting is
- Why it matters when using AI tools like Claude
- How assigning a role changes the quality of AI responses
- One example without a role prompt
- One example with a role prompt
- Three practical benefits of Role-Based Prompting

Also create a LinkedIn-ready image concept.

---

## Without Role Prompt

**Prompt:**
How can I improve my resume?

**Output:**
The response was generic and provided general advice suitable for most users.

---

## With Role Prompt

**Prompt:**
You are a Senior Software Developer.

How can I improve my resume?

**Output:**
The response focused on technical skills, GitHub, projects, DSA, coding practices, and interview preparation.

---

## Comparison

| Without Role Prompt | With Role Prompt |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Generic Answer | Expert-Level Answer |
| Basic Suggestions | Role-Specific Guidance |
| Less Detailed | More Detailed |
| General Perspective | Professional Perspective |

---

## Key Learnings

- Role-Based Prompting improves AI response quality.
- Different personas provide different perspectives.
- AI generates more accurate and specialized outputs.
- Assigning a role makes responses more practical and useful.
